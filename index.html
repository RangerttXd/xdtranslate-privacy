XdTranslate 隐私政策

适用于 Microsoft Edge 加载项商店发布的 XdTranslate 1.1.3。

本机翻译

当你发起整页翻译、正文双语或划词翻译时，XdTranslate 读取需要翻译的文字，并在设备上通过 Microsoft Edge 的本机翻译功能处理。首次使用某些语言时，浏览器可能下载语言包。

数据和设置

扩展不会将网页文字、选中文字或译文发给开发者或第三方服务，也不会记录浏览历史。原文语言、译文语言和显示方式保存在浏览器本地。你可以在扩展面板里修改这些设置，或通过卸载扩展移除设置。

XdTranslate Privacy Policy

This policy applies to XdTranslate 1.1.3 distributed through the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store.

On-device translation

When you request full-page, bilingual article, or selected-text translation, XdTranslate reads the text needed for the request and processes it on your device using Microsoft Edge’s built-in translation feature. The browser may download language packs when you first use certain languages.

Data and settings

The extension does not send page text, selected text, or translations to the developer or a third-party service. It does not record browsing history. It stores your source language, target language, and display preferences in the browser. You can change them in the extension panel or remove them by uninstalling the extension.
